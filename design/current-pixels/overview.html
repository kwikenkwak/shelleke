Overview & launcher search

A full-screen overlay per monitor over a 45%%-opaque scrim of bg; clicking the scrim or pressing Escape closes it. The column is centred horizontally and starts at 12%% of screen height, 18 px between the search widget and the workspace grid.

Search: a 560×48 panel (3 px border) with a puzzle glyph and a 16 px input placeholdered "Search, calculate or run"; results hang directly below in a second 3 px panel whose top border overlaps (negative 3 px margin), max 520 px tall. Each result row is the type label (grey, omitted for apps), the name, and — only when selected/hovered — a Silkscreen verb on the right; the active row inverts to a solid line fill with bg content. Typing anywhere routes into the field; Ctrl+J/K move the selection; Enter runs the current row. Prefixes drive apps / run / math / clipboard / emoji / web search.

Grid: 2×5 workspace tiles at 18%% of screen size (346×186 for 1920×1080), 6 px apart, 2 px grey2 borders, workspace number in Silkscreen grey2 at 40%% of tile height. Live ScreencopyView window thumbnails sit at their real positions with a 1 px border (2 px + a 12%%/30%% white wash on hover/press) and a centred grayscale app icon at 40%% of the smaller side (70%% when tiny). Drag a window onto another tile to move it; left-click focuses, middle-click closes. The focused workspace is ringed by a 5 px fg border that animates between cells in 110 ms.

Font note: the shell renders body text in Tiny5 (bitmap, PixTheme.fontMainChoice = "tiny5") and titles in Silkscreen, both bundled in assets/fonts/. These pages use a system monospace fallback stack, so glyph shapes differ — metrics, sizes, weights, spacing and colors are the real values.
