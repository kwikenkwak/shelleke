Quick Settings sidebar

Right sidebar, 360 px wide, anchored top+right+bottom (full screen height), 3 px border, 12 px padding, 11 px row gap. Closes on click-out (HyprlandFocusGrab) or Escape. Top-to-bottom: uptime chip + 4 header buttons, three toggle rows, the notification list (the only flex-growing section), a notification footer, then the calendar / to-do / timer area. Tapping the Internet or Bluetooth tile swaps the whole panel for an inline manager overlay.

Font note: the shell renders body text in Tiny5 (bitmap, PixTheme.fontMainChoice = "tiny5") and titles in Silkscreen, both bundled in assets/fonts/. These pages use a system monospace fallback stack, so glyph shapes differ — metrics, sizes, weights, spacing and colors are the real values.
