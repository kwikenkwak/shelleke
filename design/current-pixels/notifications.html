Notification popups

Toasts stack top-right on the focused screen inside a 380 px wide click-through window; each toast is 356 px wide, 3 px border, 11 px padding, 10 px apart, starting barHeight + 12 = 58 px from the top and 12 px from the right edge. Left: a 34×34 hollow square holding the grayscale app icon. Right: app name + relative time, a bold single-line summary, a grey 2-line body, then one full-width button per labelled action. Hovering cancels the auto-dismiss timer; clicking the toast fires the default action and dismisses.

Font note: the shell renders body text in Tiny5 (bitmap, PixTheme.fontMainChoice = "tiny5") and titles in Silkscreen, both bundled in assets/fonts/. These pages use a system monospace fallback stack, so glyph shapes differ — metrics, sizes, weights, spacing and colors are the real values.
