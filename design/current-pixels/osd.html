On-screen display (volume / brightness)

A single small panel centred horizontally, barHeight + 14 = 60 px below the top edge, on the focused screen. Shown for Config.options.osd.timeout (default 1000 ms) after a volume or brightness change — scrolling the bar, media keys, or IPC. Hovering it dismisses it immediately. Layout: a 21 px pixel glyph, then a label + right-aligned percent, then a discrete meter of 20 cells (9×16, 2 px border, 3 px gap) filled up to the value. Panel padding is 14 px horizontal / 11 px vertical inside a 3 px border.

Font note: the shell renders body text in Tiny5 (bitmap, PixTheme.fontMainChoice = "tiny5") and titles in Silkscreen, both bundled in assets/fonts/. These pages use a system monospace fallback stack, so glyph shapes differ — metrics, sizes, weights, spacing and colors are the real values.
