Media controls & lyrics

Anchored to the top-left, barHeight + 6 = 52 px down, flush with the left screen edge. Card is 460 px wide with a 3 px border; the layer surface keeps a fixed height (170 player + 210 lyrics) while only the card animates between 170 px and 380 px over 300 ms OutCubic, revealing the lyrics. Closes on click-out or Escape.

Player row: a 142×142 album-art frame (2 px border) — the single sanctioned color in the whole shell, drawn unsmoothed — then title (Silkscreen 16) with a lyrics-toggle button, artist in grey, elapsed/total time, a discrete 7×12 px progress cell row (3 px gaps, click to seek), and prev / play-pause / next. Lyrics auto-scroll to keep the active line centred; the current line is bold 16 px fg, past lines grey2, upcoming lines grey.

Font note: the shell renders body text in Tiny5 (bitmap, PixTheme.fontMainChoice = "tiny5") and titles in Silkscreen, both bundled in assets/fonts/. These pages use a system monospace fallback stack, so glyph shapes differ — metrics, sizes, weights, spacing and colors are the real values.
