Shared widget catalog

Every pixel surface is assembled from this handful of primitives in modules/pixel/widgets/. The whole interaction model is one rule: a filled square means active/used, a hollow bordered square means inactive/free, and anything that fills flips its content colour to bg.

Font note: the shell renders body text in Tiny5 (bitmap, PixTheme.fontMainChoice = "tiny5") and titles in Silkscreen, both bundled in assets/fonts/. These pages use a system monospace fallback stack, so glyph shapes differ — metrics, sizes, weights, spacing and colors are the real values.

PixAppIcon
fallback glyph
Any app / tray / notification icon: decoded at 16 px, upscaled nearest-neighbour and fully desaturated so no app contributes colour. Falls back to a puzzle (or message) glyph when the icon cannot be resolved.
PixBatteryGlyph
18×11 body with a 2 px border, proportional inner fill, and a 2×5 nub — plus a bolt overlay while charging. u scales the whole glyph in whole pixels.
