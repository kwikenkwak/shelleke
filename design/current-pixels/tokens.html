Design tokens

The whole theme lives in modules/pixel/common/PixTheme.qml. It is strictly monochrome: two colours plus two greys, inverted between light and dark. Dark mode is the default and follows the shell-wide toggle (Appearance.m3colors.darkmode) — the pixel family reads nothing else from the Material-You palette, so the wallpaper-derived accent colours the ii family uses never reach it. The one sanctioned colour in the entire shell is album art in the media controls.

Font note: the shell renders body text in Tiny5 (bitmap, PixTheme.fontMainChoice = "tiny5") and titles in Silkscreen, both bundled in assets/fonts/. These pages use a system monospace fallback stack, so glyph shapes differ — metrics, sizes, weights, spacing and colors are the real values.
