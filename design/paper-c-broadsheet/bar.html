Paper C — Broadsheet: bar & bar popups

The bar is a 42 px strip of opaque paper with an Oxford rule along its bottom edge: tray, hairline, agate stats, the current track in italic on the left; ten ruled workspace cells in the centre; the clock in oldstyle figures, the utility glyphs and the battery on the right. Scrolling the left half changes brightness and the right half volume, exactly as today. Hovering the clock, the battery or the stat cluster opens the popups below.

Fonts. Display / titles / numerals: TeX Gyre Pagella (Palatino lineage; real smcp small caps, onum oldstyle and tnum tabular figures) — installed here, so these previews show the actual face. Data / labels: Lato. Tabular micro-data, paths and logs: Source Code Pro. All three are libre and packaged on Fedora; the shell bundles them in assets/fonts/ exactly as the pixel family bundles its bitmap faces.
