Paper C — Broadsheet: on-screen display

One small floating sheet, horizontally centred, 56 px from the top edge, shown for a second after a volume or brightness change and dismissed immediately on hover.

Fonts. Display / titles / numerals: TeX Gyre Pagella (Palatino lineage; real smcp small caps, onum oldstyle and tnum tabular figures) — installed here, so these previews show the actual face. Data / labels: Lato. Tabular micro-data, paths and logs: Source Code Pro. All three are libre and packaged on Fedora; the shell bundles them in assets/fonts/ exactly as the pixel family bundles its bitmap faces.

The OSD is the ruled gauge at its purest: a hairline baseline, ticks every 5% with a major tick every 25%, and a 4px ink bar overprinted up to the value. It replaces the twenty discrete cells of the pixel family with a measuring scale, which is both more precise and quieter.
