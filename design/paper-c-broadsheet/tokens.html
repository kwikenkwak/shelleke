Paper C — Broadsheet: tokens

Every colour, face, size, rule weight, glyph and duration in the Broadsheet variant. Broadsheet is the richest of the three paper variants: it keeps the warm sheet and hairline structure of A and B but adds real typographic ornament — double and Oxford rules, corner ticks, letterspaced small-cap section heads, oldstyle figures, sepia seals — and a two-colour editorial accent. It is still airy: the ornament is always thin, and it always marks structure rather than decorating it.

Fonts. Display / titles / numerals: TeX Gyre Pagella (Palatino lineage; real smcp small caps, onum oldstyle and tnum tabular figures) — installed here, so these previews show the actual face. Data / labels: Lato. Tabular micro-data, paths and logs: Source Code Pro. All three are libre and packaged on Fedora; the shell bundles them in assets/fonts/ exactly as the pixel family bundles its bitmap faces.

Round caps and joins, no fills except the wifi dot and the transport triangles. The stroke never scales: a 40px session glyph is drawn with the same 1.25px line as a 12px row glyph, which is what makes the set read as engraving rather than UI.

Elevation, radius, opacity
TokenValueWhere
--r-ctl2pxcontrols, chips, slots, fields. Panels and sheets stay at 0 — a sheet of paper is cut, not rounded. Medallions and seals are full circles.
--shade0 1px 1px rgba(36,31,26,.05), 0 10px 26px rgba(36,31,26,.09)floating surfaces only (popups, toasts, OSD, dialogs). Always paired with a 1px edge, because the edge does the work and the shadow only lifts.
--shade-sm0 1px 2px rgba(36,31,26,.07)tooltips, album plate
grainfractal noise, 0.055 alpha, multiply, 140px tileevery paper ground and the wallpaper band. Invisible until you look for it; it is what keeps large flat areas from reading as plastic.
scrimrgba(28,24,19,.72) / dusk rgba(8,7,5,.78)session screen, worktrees dialog. Overview uses .55.
disabledcolour → --ink-4, border → dottednever opacity on a whole control — a dotted rule reads as “not available” the way a dotted line does on a form

Nothing in Broadsheet slides very far. State changes are washes and rule weights, so 90–160 ms is enough; only real geometry (the media card growing lyrics) gets 280.
