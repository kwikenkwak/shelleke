Paper C — Broadsheet: media controls

Anchored to the top-left corner, 52 px below the bar, 460 px wide. Album plate, title and artist, elapsed / total, a seekable progress rule and the transport row; the lyrics toggle grows the card from 172 to 388 px.

Fonts. Display / titles / numerals: TeX Gyre Pagella (Palatino lineage; real smcp small caps, onum oldstyle and tnum tabular figures) — installed here, so these previews show the actual face. Data / labels: Lato. Tabular micro-data, paths and logs: Source Code Pro. All three are libre and packaged on Fedora; the shell bundles them in assets/fonts/ exactly as the pixel family bundles its bitmap faces.

The progress bar is the ruled gauge again, minus its ticks: a hairline baseline, a 2px oxblood bar and a 1px oxblood upright at the play head. Click anywhere on it to seek. Lyrics are Pagella; the current line is 16 px ink with a 2px oxblood change bar, past lines fade to --ink-4 and upcoming lines sit at --ink-3.
