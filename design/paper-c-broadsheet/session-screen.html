Paper C — Broadsheet: session screen

A full-screen, keyboard-exclusive overlay: six 128 px tiles over a warm scrim, number accelerators, the hint line and any session warnings as stamps.

Fonts. Display / titles / numerals: TeX Gyre Pagella (Palatino lineage; real smcp small caps, onum oldstyle and tnum tabular figures) — installed here, so these previews show the actual face. Data / labels: Lato. Tabular micro-data, paths and logs: Source Code Pro. All three are libre and packaged on Fedora; the shell bundles them in assets/fonts/ exactly as the pixel family bundles its bitmap faces.

The scrim is warm near-black at 72%, so the wallpaper stays legible as texture. Six 128 px sheets of paper are laid on it with their own shadow; each carries its number accelerator in oldstyle figures in the top-left corner, a 38 px glyph, and its name in Pagella small caps. The selected tile does not invert — it takes an oxblood frame, oxblood corner ticks, an oxblood glyph and an oxblood label, which is both quieter and easier to follow when you arrow across the row.
