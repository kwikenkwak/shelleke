Paper A — “Hairline” · widget catalog

Every primitive the shell needs, rebuilt without a single filled control. The pixel family had exactly one interaction rule — filled square = on. Hairline replaces it with three, in this order of loudness: ink weight (ink-3 → ink), a 1 px underline, and a 6 px dot. Nothing else is permitted to say “on”.

Type: the shell sets body/UI in Inter (google-inter-fonts / ttf-inter) and every numeral, identifier and path in JetBrains Mono (already installed here). These pages fall back to Adwaita Sans — an Inter derivative — when Inter is absent, so shapes are within a hair of the real thing; all sizes, weights, tracking, colours and metrics are the real values.

1 · Surface — PaperSurface

Replaces PixPanel. Opaque paper ground, a single 1 px rule border, radius 0, no shadow, no second border weight — the pixel family’s 2 px/3 px distinction is gone. A surface that touches a screen edge drops the border on that edge and keeps only the inboard hairline (the bar keeps its bottom rule and nothing else).

3 · Buttons — PaperButton

Replaces PixButton. There is no box and no fill state; a button is its label. Three shapes: caps (destructive / primary / dialog), text (inline, secondary), icon (24 × 24 hit area, 16 px glyph, no visible bounds). Hit areas stay ≥ 24 px even though nothing is drawn there.

5 · Field — PaperField

Replaces PixField. A micro-caps label, the value, and a single hairline underneath — no box, ever. Rest rule-2, focus ink plus a 1 px caret, invalid alert with the message directly beneath in alert at 11 px. Selection is a wash-2 band, never an ink fill.

6 · Meters & progress

The 20-cell discrete bar is gone. A meter is a 1 px rule-2 track with a 1 px ink fill and a 4 px head dot — the head is what makes a 1 px line readable at a glance, and it is also the seek handle in the media card.

Battery glyph — PaperBattery

78 %41 %9 %

18 × 9 hairline shell with a 2 px nub. The level fill is the one solid ink mark allowed to exceed 6 px, because it encodes a quantity rather than a state. ≤ 15 % turns the whole glyph and its percent alert-red.
