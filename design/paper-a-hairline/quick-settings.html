Type: the shell sets body/UI in Inter (google-inter-fonts / ttf-inter) and every numeral, identifier and path in JetBrains Mono (already installed here). Every bordered tile from the pixel build is gone. The six connectivity controls that used to be 46 px bordered tiles in a 3 × 2 grid are now 56 px rows in a single column — same information, more of it visible, and the switch tells you the state without the title needing to invert.

The five “extras” keep their grid because they are peers, but a filled button becomes an ink glyph over an ink micro-cap with a 1 px underline. WARP and Easy Effects are on above.

Notifications keep grouping, expansion, per-item delete and relative times. The count moved into the section header, which is where every other count in the theme lives, so the pixel build’s non-interactive full-width “5 notification(s)” button disappears entirely.

The three calendar mini-buttons became a tab row sitting on the rule that already separated the section. Today is ink with an 18 px underline instead of a filled rectangle; out-of-month days are ink-4.
