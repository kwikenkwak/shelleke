Paper B · LedgerDesign tokens

The complete token sheet for Paper B “Ledger” — colour in both palettes, the type scale and its three faces, spacing, rule weights, radii, the icon grid, and motion. Everything downstream in this folder is assembled out of exactly these values; no surface introduces a one-off colour, size or weight.

Fonts. Titles and numerals-with-personality are set in Bitstream Charter (Matthew Carter’s screen-tuned transitional serif, shipped by xorg-x11-fonts/bitstream-charter-fonts on Fedora). UI text is Adwaita Sans (Fedora’s Inter-derived system sans). All figures, identifiers and tabular data are Source Code Pro. Every one of the three is installed on this machine, so these previews render in the real faces — sizes, weights, tracking and colour are the shipping values.

Philosophy

The idea

A shell that behaves like a well-kept ledger. Warm paper ground with a barely-there grain, ink text, and structure carried entirely by 1 px hairlines — card borders, row separators, section rules, dotted leaders. Data lines up in columns the way bookkeeping does: label left in letterspaced small caps, figure right in a monospace with tabular figures, a dotted leader joining them. Nothing shouts. The page is quiet, the marks are precise.

Rules of the hand
  • Hairlines, not fills. Structure is drawn, not blocked in.
  • Two inks. Ink blue means you can act on this / this is on. Stamp red means this is a warning or a record you cannot undo. Nothing else is coloured.
  • Hover is a wash (one step of paper), selection is a mark (a 2 px underline or a 2 px left rule), never a heavy fill.
  • One whisper of shadow, only on surfaces that actually float over the desktop.
  • Small and precise. 13 px body, 10 px caps labels, 16 px icons.

Third-party colour

App, tray and window icons keep their own artwork but pass through grayscale(.55) contrast(.95) and sit on a hairline plate, so a pasted-in logo reads like a stamp on the page rather than a sticker. Album art is the one exception and renders at full saturation — the ledger is allowed one colour plate.

Palette — Dusk Paper (dark)

Same structure, inverted ground. The dark palette is warm charcoal, never black, and ink is warm bone, never white — the page reads as paper under a desk lamp. Note the one logic inversion: --paper-sunk is darker than the card in dusk, so a hover wash still reads as “pressed into the sheet”.

Swatches are shown on paper so the dark values stay comparable; the live sample beside it is the real dusk ground.

Type scale

Three faces, three jobs, no overlap. Charter (serif) titles only — it is what makes the surface feel like a bound book instead of a control panel. Adwaita Sans carries every label, row and paragraph. Source Code Pro carries every figure, identifier, path and timestamp, always with tabular-nums, so columns of numbers align down the page.

Icon system

One coherent set of 65 thin-stroke line icons, drawn as inline SVG on a 16×16 grid with a 1.25 unit stroke, round caps and round joins, all content inside a 1.6 unit margin so nothing touches the box. Optical sizes are 12 (inline meta), 14 (rows, buttons, bar), 16 (tiles, headers), 20 (OSD, search), 28 (session tiles). Colour is currentColor: --ink-2 at rest, --ink when the row is active, --blue when the control is on.

Animated properties are restricted to opacity, colour, and position under 4 px. Nothing scales, nothing bounces, nothing blurs. A selection mark grows from 0 to 2 px in 140 ms; that is the largest movement in the shell.

Ground & texture

paperdesktop ground & bar body
paper + grainevery large surface, 3% fractal noise, multiply
paper-raisecards, panels, popups
paper-sunkwells, hover, log boxes

The grain is a single inline fractal-noise SVG at 3% opacity in light mode and 5.5% in dusk, multiplied over the ground. It is deliberately below the threshold of notice at arm’s length — you feel it, you do not see it. In QML it is one cached ShaderEffectSource tile reused by every surface.
