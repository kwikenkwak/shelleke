Paper B · LedgerDisplays overlay

A PanelWindow anchored top+left+bottom over the full screen height, 360 px wide, keybind/IPC only. It is a GUI over hyprdynamicmonitors: every write goes through the Monitors service to hdm-control.py. Three screens — main, profile editor, settings — the latter two replacing the body in place rather than floating over it. This is the most data-dense surface in the shell and the one that most wants the ledger treatment: caps section rules, dotted key/value leaders, and figures in mono so two outputs can be compared down a column.

Fonts. Titles and numerals-with-personality are set in Bitstream Charter (Matthew Carter’s screen-tuned transitional serif, shipped by xorg-x11-fonts/bitstream-charter-fonts on Fedora). UI text is Adwaita Sans (Fedora’s Inter-derived system sans). All figures, identifiers and tabular data are Source Code Pro. Every one of the three is installed on this machine, so these previews render in the real faces — sizes, weights, tracking and colour are the shipping values.
